Frequently asked questions

What kind of products can I find on Cirrus?

You'll discover a thoughtful selection of products across various categories, all chosen for their quality and unique appeal. We focus on items that spark joy and fit well into everyday life.

Is Cirrus free to use?

Yes, browsing products, joining communities, and interacting with others on Cirrus is completely free. You only pay for the items you choose to purchase.

How do I connect with other users?

You can join various interest-based communities, comment on posts, and follow other members whose tastes align with yours. We encourage friendly and respectful interaction.

How does shopping work on Cirrus?

When you find something you like, you can add it to your basket and complete your purchase securely through our platform. We work with trusted payment providers for a smooth transaction.

Can I sell my own products on Cirrus?

Currently, Cirrus is focused on helping you discover and shop from our curated selection of sellers. We might explore options for new sellers in the future.

How are products shipped?

Shipping details and costs are provided by individual sellers for each product. You'll see estimated delivery times and any associated charges clearly before you complete your purchase.

What is your return policy?

Our return policy is designed to be fair and straightforward. You can find detailed information on returns and refunds in our dedicated section, or contact our support team for specific queries.
